What it is about

How artificial intelligence is revolutionizing the shopping process and customer service. Are you ready for the new customer?

A new year, a familiar duo – and a topic that affects us all: What happens when artificial intelligence becomes a game changer not only for companies but also for customers? In this episode, we discuss how AI completely redefines the shopping process, consultation, and even complaints, and why companies now need to prepare for data-savvy, well-equipped AI customers.

We share our own experiences from everyday life, take a look at current figures, and venture predictions on how AI will act as a shopping and negotiation agent in the future. It's not just about smart product advice, but also about agent-to-agent communication, new challenges for companies, and the crucial role of trust in a world where machines act on our behalf. Tune in and find out how you can prepare for tomorrow's customer!
