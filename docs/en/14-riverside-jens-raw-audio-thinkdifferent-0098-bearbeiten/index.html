What it is about

From Neo to Optimus: How robots could change our lives. Science fiction meets everyday life – and we're testing the limits.

In this episode, we take a close look at the latest developments around household robots and ask ourselves: Has the future already moved in with us? We discuss the newest models like Neo and Optimus, their capabilities, prices, and what really lies behind the shiny promotional videos. We discuss how much true autonomy is already possible, why operators in the background often still pull the strings, and what opportunities and risks this whole thing poses for our everyday lives. Of course, we also address the big questions surrounding data protection, security, and acceptance – and tell how science fiction suddenly becomes reality. In the end, we ask ourselves: Would we order a robot ourselves – and what does all this mean for our lives?!
