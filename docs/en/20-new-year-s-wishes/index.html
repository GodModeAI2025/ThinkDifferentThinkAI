What it is about

How Smart Glasses, robotics, and agents could shape our next year. A glance with a wink and foresight.

We look ahead together and ask ourselves: What do we wish for the AI year 2026? In this episode, we reflect on Christmas, thank everyone who works even during the holidays, and take you on a journey through our personal visions for the future regarding artificial intelligence. From smart glasses and voice-first interfaces to robotics for everyday life – we discuss which technologies are already tangible and which developments we truly wish for.

In doing so, we remain critical, humorous, and close to your reality of life. We talk about data protection, EU regulations, new interaction patterns, and why AI shouldn't answer everything immediately. And of course, we wonder what it takes for AI solutions to really become useful and accessible for everyone. Tune in if you want to know how we envision the AI year 2026 – with all its opportunities, challenges, and a touch of future optimism.
