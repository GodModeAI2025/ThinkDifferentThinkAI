What it is about

From dog cancer cures to OpenClaw revolution. Why every company needs AI agents now – and what leaves us speechless.

We share our genuine moments of realization, doubts, and hopes – always in an authentic dialogue. Tune in if you want to know why we are witnessing a true paradigm shift right now.
