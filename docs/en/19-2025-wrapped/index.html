What it is about

An honest year in review of the most exciting AI tools, trends, and challenges. What stays, what comes, what surprises?

Together we take a critical look at the AI tool landscape in 2025: What has really proven itself, and where are we still far from the big promise? In this episode, we discuss which AI applications excite us, which trends surprise us, and how our workflow has changed over the year. We speak openly about Google, OpenAI, Apple, Amazon, and all the small and big players who are shaking up the market – or not. We share our personal experiences, Aha moments, and also the stumbling blocks we've encountered. Anyone wanting to know how AI really works in everyday life and where it's headed is in the right place here.
