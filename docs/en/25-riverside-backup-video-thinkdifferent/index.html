What it is about

Guest: Martin Jäger (Huntercoach.de)

In this episode, together with Martin Jäger, AI and automation consultant, we take a close look at the German Mittelstand. We openly discuss why technical progress is much more than buzzwords – and why it takes courage right now to avoid being left behind. Martin shares his personal story of how AI has removed obstacles and opened new paths for him, and he dispels myths surrounding digitalization, education, and willingness to change. We discuss why expertise alone is no longer enough and how companies can leverage real opportunities instead of waiting for the next wave. The result: An honest look at challenges but also at the enormous potential that AI holds for each of us.
