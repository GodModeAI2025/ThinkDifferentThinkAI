What it is about

When artificial intelligence meets warmth of heart: Christmas stories, reflections, and real surprises surrounding AI.

In this special Christmas episode, we take you on a journey between magic and technology. We tell festive AI stories, discuss soulless sleigh rides, and show how modern technology can change gift-giving. Together we question whether machines really understand magic – and where heart and humanity remain irreplaceable. Our conversations arise, as always, spontaneously and authentically, directly from the moment. Celebrate with us the fusion of innovation, feeling, and true Christmas joy – and discover what AI really means at Christmas.
