What it is about

How AI flattens hierarchies

Join Jens and Mark as they explore the evolving landscape of AI in the workplace.

This episode discusses the challenges and opportunities AI presents, from changing job roles to increasing productivity. They discuss the importance of soft skills, the potential of AI to create new roles, and the necessity of an AI-first mentality.

With engaging anecdotes and expert insights, this episode provides a comprehensive look at how AI is shaping the future of work and society.
