What it is about

How smart AI agents are redefining our everyday lives and the working world. A look at China – and into our future.

In this episode, we take you into the fast-paced world of AI agents and show how OpenClaw & Co. are changing the rules of the game. We discuss why a real public festival is emerging around local AI installations in China, while we in Germany are still thinking about security and regulation.

We share personal experiences with setting up our own AI agents, talk about international competition, new business models, and how our perception of technology is changing. Whether it's a birthday card or a business process – we show how AI opens up new possibilities for us and why openness and curiosity are crucial.
