What it is about

Easter Eggs, AI fails, and why artificial intelligence sometimes thinks like we do. An Easter special full of aha moments.

In this special Easter episode, we take you on a journey to the most bizarre and exciting Easter Eggs – from legendary video games to surprising quirks of modern AI. We share personal stories, expose AI fails, and discuss why even the smartest systems sometimes act like forgetful, agreeable, or just plain 'lazy' humans.

Together we question how much humanity is in the machine, what AI can really do – and where it still makes us smile. Whether you're AI pros or just curious: this episode gives you new perspectives, entertaining anecdotes, and plenty of conversation starters for the holidays. Happy Easter and enjoy listening and egg hunting!
