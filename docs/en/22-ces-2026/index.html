What it is about

AI gets a body

We take you on a journey through the highlights of this year's Consumer Electronics Show in this special episode – even though we weren't live on site. AI was omnipresent, robotics fascinated with new forms of movement, and even households are getting an upgrade thanks to smart gadgets. We discuss what lies behind the innovations, which developments are truly meaningful, and where marketing promises more than the technology can deliver. Particularly in focus: the merging of artificial intelligence and robotics, new health gadgets, and the triumph of smart sensors. Tune in if you want to know which trends really have potential – and which ones are more for a chuckle.
